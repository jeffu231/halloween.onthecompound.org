Another year has gone by and we are once again making preparations for the holiday lighting season. Halloween prep has begun with some new songs and updates to your old favorites. There are more houses on the lane this 
year with some of them planning to decorate for Halloween. We hope to see you. The show is tentatively set to begin around Oct 4 this year.

Once again, you will be able to vote for your favorite song to be played. While a song is playing, the voting round opens and anyone with the link to the song voting page can vote for what song plays next. You can vote for any song in our playlist. The site will also show you the song and artist currently playing, as well as what's up next if no one votes up another song. The show hours are also listed.

Be sure to allow the website to use your location. We only allow votes from people that are actually at the show.
